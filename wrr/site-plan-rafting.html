Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this website is to introduce YOU to the exciting world of white water rafting and make it easy to get started! This site will provide helpful information on what to expect, how to prepare, and how to book your own adventure with Cary's River Rush. It also helps our company connect with new adventurers and grow our rafting community!

Audience

The target audience for Cary's River Rush includes young adults, couples, families, and friend groups who enjoy outdoor activities and are looking for a fun and exciting experience! Many of these visitors may be first-time rafters with a moderate budget, looking for a safe but thrilling adventure to try during a vacation or weekend trip. They want clear information on what to expect, pricing, safety, and how to book easily. Their needs include feeling confident that rafting is safe, beginner-friendly, and worth the cost. Most users will likely access the site on their mobile devices while planning trips or searching for activities, so the website should be easy to navigate, visually engaging, and quick to load.
